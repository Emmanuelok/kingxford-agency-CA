    Explain the rule, not just the result. +

    Low-priority items can bypass Review when fast-track is enabled. High-priority items always pass through Review. Each cycle advances an eligible item one stage.

    Make room for a human decision. +

    With final approval enabled, Ready items wait until you approve them. The following cycle completes them. Turn the rule off to see the difference.

    Keep the demonstration honest. +

    This workspace runs entirely in your browser. It does not connect to a project management service, send messages, use AI or store data on a server.
