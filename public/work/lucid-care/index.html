A FEW THINGS, MADE CLEAR

Room for
questions.

Is Lucid Care a real clinic?

No. Lucid Care is a sample Avalon Creatives Group concept demonstrating accessible health information design. It does not provide clinical care.

Does this tool save my information?

Your checklist selections and saved guides stay in this browser after a refresh. You can clear them in My plan. No account, personal details or health information are requested.

What can I do with my checklist?

Use “Save my checklist” to download a plain-text copy of the demo preparation list. It is an organisation aid, not medical guidance.
